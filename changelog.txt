Date: 2026.07.30
  Features:
    - Updated for 2.1
    - Added loader support
  Optimizations:
    - Improved performance when circuit signals remain unchanged (thanks Mr00Anderson)
    - Other minor performance improvements
  Bugfixes:
    - Fixed this mod not working with some modded inserters and splitters
    - Fixed the batch size setting not being restored when loading saves or joining multiplayer games
    - Fixed "Set filters with quality signals" not being saved to blueprints in the blueprint library or copied when pasting blueprint settings
    - Fixed "Set filters with quality signals" not being retained during fast replacement on space platforms
    - Fixed "Set filters with quality signals" being shown for inserter ghosts that do not support filters
    - Fixed "Set filters with quality signals" being shown for modded inserters and splitters without circuit connections
    - Fixed "Set filters with quality signals" failing to save when another GUI-close event fired first (fix by Mr00Anderson)
    - Fixed red and green circuit signals being combined incorrectly when either signal was negative
    - Fixed native and proxy signals for the same quality creating duplicate filters. Their values are now summed.
